Assignment assumptions & how ownership works

Assumption: TDC has one shared people database. Paying TDC clients have an assigned matchmaker. Other profiles are sourced from services such as Shaadi and Jeevansathi, or through referrals. Source tags in this prototype are fictional provenance; no website is scraped or connected.

A client can also appear as a candidate for another client. A matchmaker owns a client relationship, not a separate candidate database. Historical sends record the operator who sent them, which may differ from the current assigned owner.

The operator dropdown simulates staff context and attribution. It is not login or access control. This build shares data across its own screens and operators in one browser; separate devices need a real backend to share changes. Browser storage is optional and wrapped in error handling.
